Good to know
🌡Weather (late Nov): highs ~65–70°F, lows ~40–45°F. Dress in layers; it is cold at night and in canyon shade. Check the forecast the week before.
⛽No cell service across most of the park. Download offline maps (Google Maps / Gaia). Fuel at Stovepipe Wells, Furnace Creek and Panamint Springs only.
🚧Closed: Scotty’s Castle and Bonnie Clare Rd (flood recovery, reopening date unknown). Titus Canyon Rd is scheduled to close Oct 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027 for repairs. Not part of this plan.
🎒Pack: 1 gal water/person/day, snacks, sunscreen, hats, layers, headlamps, a small cooler, card games and a stargazing app.

Day 0 · Wednesday Nov 25
Road trip + Alabama Hills
San Ramon → Lone Pine · sunset among the boulders
ℹ️Optional but recommended: breaking up the ~8–9 hr drive from San Ramon lets you arrive in the park rested, with a fun morning in the Alabama Hills on the way in. Pick any Lone Pine / Independence motel. Thanksgiving-week rooms go fast, so book now.
Schedule
~8:00 am
Leave San Ramon
I-580 E → I-205 → CA-99 S → Bakersfield → CA-58 E → CA-14 N → US-395 N to Lone Pine (~7 hrs of driving). Check Caltrans quickmap.dot.ca.gov that morning; the Tehachapi pass can see snow in late Nov.
~12:30 pm
Lunch break: Mojave / Inyokern / Ridgecrest
Fill the tank in Ridgecrest or Olancha. Gas inside the park is much pricier.
~3:30 pm
Arrive Lone Pine, check in
Dow Villa Motel, Frontier Motel, or similar. Confirm the friends have their rooms too.
4:00 pm
Alabama Hills golden hour
Movie Road + Mobius Arch Loop (0.6 mi, easy). Kids can scramble the rounded boulders while the Sierra glows pink at sunset (~4:45 pm). Mt. Whitney is the peak behind you.
6:00 pm
Dinner in Lone Pine
Alabama Hills Cafe, Seasons Restaurant, or Lone Pine Pizza. Early night: tomorrow is travel + Thanksgiving.
Day 1 · Thursday Nov 26 · Thanksgiving
Arrive + Thanksgiving dinner
Lone Pine → Stovepipe Wells · dunes at sunset · turkey at the Toll Road
🦃Thanksgiving dinner: Toll Road Restaurant (the on-site restaurant at Stovepipe Wells) is a short walk from your room. Call (760) 786-7090 or the front desk now to reserve for 6 and to confirm the Thanksgiving menu and seating times. I could not confirm a published holiday menu online.
Schedule
8:00 am
Lone Pine → Stovepipe Wells
CA-136 E → CA-190 E, about 1 hr 45 min. Gas up in Lone Pine first (nothing else until Panamint Springs).
~9:15 am
Father Crowley Vista Point
Father Crowley Vista Point overlooks Rainbow Canyon, where military jets sometimes fly below you. Quick stop. Then the long descent into Panamint Valley.
~10:30 am
Arrive Stovepipe Wells Village
Ask for early check-in; otherwise drop bags and explore. Stock the room cooler at the General Store (no in-room kitchens).
12:00 pm
Easy lunch
Toll Road Restaurant or box lunches from the General Store.
1:30 pm
Pool + rest
Quiet hour after the drive. Kids can burn energy walking the village loop.
3:45 pm
Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes at sunset
2 mi from the hotel. Walk up the main dune (~1 mi, soft sand, no trail). Sunset ~4:50 pm; bring shoes you do not mind filling with sand. Great late light for photos of the whole family.
6:00 pm
🦃 Thanksgiving dinner: Toll Road Restaurant
Pre-book for your group of 6. Casual Old West décor, a classic turkey-and-trimmings style meal is typical, but verify the menu and price (≈$30–45 pp likely; confirm).
8:00 pm
Moonlit dunes or stargazing
The moon is nearly full around Thanksgiving, so skies will be bright. Moonlit walks on the dunes are the trade-off. Lights-out stargazing improves each night after.
Day 2 · Friday Nov 27
Badlands + Badwater
Zabriskie Point · Golden Canyon loop · Artists Palette · lowest point in North America
⚠️This is the big adventure day: about 4 hrs of driving and a 4-mile hike. Pack 1 gal water per person, snacks, sun protection, and layers. Fill the tank at Furnace Creek (30 min from Stovepipe). Check NPS conditions page that morning for trail and road status.
Schedule
7:00 am
Breakfast at Toll Road
Breakfast buffet starts 7 am.
7:45 am
Drive to Zabriskie Point (~35 min)
Morning light on the golden badlands, and Manly Beacon. Short paved walk up from the lot.
8:45 am
Golden Canyon → Gower Gulch Loop (4.3 mi, ~800 ft gain)
The strenuous highlight. Up Golden Canyon to the Red Cathedral cliffs, over the badlands ridge, back down Gower Gulch. Allow 3 hrs with kids. Shorter bail-out: Golden Canyon out-and-back to Red Cathedral (~3 mi round trip).
12:15 pm
Lunch at Furnace Creek
Furnace Creek Ranch has casual options (Last Kick Saloon / general store). Concessionaire changed recently, so verify hours. Or picnic from Stovepipe.
1:30 pm
Artists Drive + Artists Palette
9-mile one-way loop. Multi-colored hills of volcanic minerals. Park at the Palette and wander.
2:30 pm
Devil’s Golf Course
Jagged salt formations, listen for the crackle as they expand in heat. Short walk.
3:15 pm
Natural Bridge
1 mi round trip, easy. Kids love the rock arch overhead.
3:55 pm
Badwater Basin (−282 ft)
North America’s lowest point. Walk out a mile on the salt flats for the polygons. Golden light before sunset (~4:50 pm).
4:45 pm
Drive back to Stovepipe (~1 hr 15 min)
Roads are fine at dusk, but watch for wildlife.
6:30 pm
Dinner + pool/hot shower
Toll Road Restaurant or Badwater Saloon (pub fare, kid-friendly).
ℹ️Telescope Peak (14 mi RT, 3,000+ ft) is not recommended for the kids in late November; the high-elevation trail often has snow and ice and the 4WD road to the trailhead may be closed.
Day 3 · Saturday Nov 28
Slow day + dark skies
Mosaic Canyon · pool · stargazing
🌿Lighter day: one great morning hike near the hotel, a long afternoon break, and a dark-sky evening.
Schedule
8:30 am
Breakfast, no rush
9:30 am
Mosaic Canyon (up to 4 mi RT)
15 min from the hotel; 2.25 mi graded dirt road, passable by sedan (ask a ranger if it has rained). Polished marble walls, then a scramble over the boulder jam at ~1.3 mi. Kids can turn around at the narrows; adults can push to the dryfall.
12:30 pm
Lunch + pool time
Spa-fed pool and loungers. Bring books and cards for a real break.
2:30 pm
Optional: Salt Creek + Harmony Borax Works
Salt Creek (0.5 mi boardwalk) has pupfish, and Harmony Borax Works has old 20-mule-team wagons. About 30 min each way. Easy and educational for the kids; skip if everyone wants to relax.
5:30 pm
Dinner
Toll Road Restaurant or Badwater Saloon.
6:45 pm
Stargazing
Death Valley is an International Dark Sky Park. The waning moon rises later each night, giving a dark window early in the evening. Walk out to the dunes parking lot or the field next to the hotel. Download a stargazing app (Stellarium or SkyView) beforehand. Bring jackets and blankets.
